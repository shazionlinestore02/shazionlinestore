Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about shopping and ordering at ShaziOnlineStore.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Yes. Cash on Delivery is available for eligible orders across Pakistan. You can pay when you receive your order.
How can I place an order?
Choose your favourite product, click the Order Now button, enter your name, phone number, city and complete delivery address, then submit your order.
Where do you deliver?
We offer delivery across Pakistan. Delivery availability may vary depending on the customer's location.
How long does delivery take?
Delivery normally takes approximately 3–5 days.
Is return available?
Yes. Returns are allowed within 7 days, according to the applicable return policy.
How will I know that my order is confirmed?
After you submit your order, our team will process your order details. If any confirmation is required, we may contact you using the phone number provided.
Can I order more than one product?
Yes. You can order the products available on our website by submitting an order for the items you want.
Is online payment required?
No. Cash on Delivery is available, so you can pay when you receive your order.
